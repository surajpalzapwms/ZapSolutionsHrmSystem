Privacy Policy

App: H.R.M. In A ZAP (com.zapsolutionz.hrm) · Developer: Zap Solutionz · Last updated: 19 October 2026

H.R.M. In A ZAP ("the App") is a human resource management app used by employees and administrators of organisations registered with our service. This policy explains what data the App collects, why, and how it is protected.

1. Who can use the App

The App is intended only for employees and administrators of registered organisations. Accounts are created by the organisation's administrator. The App is not intended for children under 18.

2. Data we collect

  • Account and profile information: name, employee ID, email address, phone number, date of birth, designation and profile photo.
  • Precise location (including in the background): used only to automatically record attendance – your in-time when you arrive at the office and going-time when you leave. Location is checked against your office location and is not used for any other purpose.
  • Wi-Fi and network information: the name of the Wi-Fi network you are connected to, used to confirm you are at the office for attendance.
  • Attendance and leave records: sign-in/sign-out times, late coming, leave requests and holidays.
  • Salary and payroll information: salary, advances, deductions and settlements, which your employer records for you.
  • Photos and documents: images or files you choose to upload (for example a profile picture or HR documents), using your camera or storage only when you choose to.

3. How we use your data

  • To record and show your attendance automatically.
  • To manage leave, payroll, letters, documents and expenses for your organisation.
  • To let your organisation's administrators view and manage staff records.
  • To keep the App secure and working properly.

We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell your data.

4. Background location

With your permission, the App uses your location while the App is closed or not in use, so attendance can be marked automatically when you arrive at or leave the office. A notification is shown while this is active. You can turn off background location at any time in your phone's settings; you will then need to mark attendance manually.

5. Sharing of data

Your data is visible only to you and to authorised administrators of your own organisation. We use trusted service providers to operate the App (for example, cloud hosting and image storage). They process data only on our behalf and may not use it for their own purposes. We do not share your data with advertisers or other third parties, except where required by law.

6. Data security

All data is sent between the App and our servers over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Access to data is restricted by login and by role.

7. Data retention and deletion

We keep your data for as long as your organisation uses our service or as required for payroll and legal records. You can ask your organisation's administrator, or contact us at the email below, to delete your account and associated data. We will process deletion requests within 30 days, except for records we are legally required to keep.

8. Your choices

  • You can deny or revoke location, camera and photo permissions in your phone settings at any time.
  • You can request a copy, correction or deletion of your data by contacting us.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this page.
